Deeni Students and AI

A live online lecture by Soban Attari for Dars e Nizami Jamia students — brothers and sisters — on making AI a trustworthy companion for your Islamic studies.

Overview

What Is Deeni Students and AI?

An online session delivered by Soban Attari to students of Dars e Nizami. It is a practical walkthrough of how AI can support serious Islamic study — used carefully, with the right adab and the right checks.

📖

AI in Your Islamic Studies

Using AI to search, summarise, translate, and cross-check while studying fiqh, hadith, tafsir, and Arabic — and knowing where it must not be trusted.

⚡

Better Productivity with AI

Organising notes, revision, lesson plans, and time so more of your day goes to actual learning and less to busywork.

🏆

Becoming Excellent with AI

Raising the standard of your writing, research, and understanding, so AI lifts your work instead of replacing your effort.
