Sessions at FAST-NUCES Lahore and Chiniot-Faisalabad

Soban Attari has held several sessions at FAST-NUCES: a Milaad gathering and a session on protecting creative ideas at the Lahore campus, and a training session on professional ethics at the Chiniot-Faisalabad campus.

Photos: Soban Attari’s official Facebook page · tap a photo to view it full size

About the Session

At the Lahore campus, a Milaad gathering in the main auditorium was attended by the campus Director, Dr Amjad Hussain, along with teachers and students.

At the Chiniot-Faisalabad campus, he delivered a training session in the main auditorium on professional ethics.

He explained how sharing an idea too early can stifle creativity and invite criticism before the idea is ready, and how keeping it protected creates space for innovation and growth.

For students building projects and startups, he covered practical protections such as non-disclosure agreements and patent filings to secure intellectual property.

He encouraged students to believe in their own ideas, and to refine them with feedback from a small, trusted circle rather than the crowd.

Key Takeaways

  • Protect new ideas until they are ready.
  • Use NDAs and patents to safeguard intellectual property.
  • Seek feedback from a trusted circle, not everyone.
