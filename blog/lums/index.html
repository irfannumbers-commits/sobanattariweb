Mehfil-e-Milaad and Seerat Seminar at LUMS

Soban Attari has appeared at LUMS both as a reciter at the LUMS Religious Society’s Mehfil-e-Milaad and as a guest speaker at a Seerat and motivational seminar for students.

Photos: Soban Attari’s official Facebook page · tap a photo to view it full size

About the Session

On 27 September 2023, the LUMS Religious Society held a Mehfil-e-Milaad, described in its events record as a soulful devotional evening featuring Soban Attari in praise of the Holy Prophet ﷺ.

He has also spoken to LUMS students at a Seerat and motivational seminar on ethics, moral values, social responsibility and civic sense. His message was that the character of the Holy Prophet ﷺ is the role model for everyone and the ultimate source of guidance for every part of society.

The seminar closed with Darood-o-Salam, and the organising students presented him with a souvenir.

Posts on his Facebook page also record Milaad gatherings in the LUMS main auditorium, where he delivered a talk on the Sunnah to students.

Key Takeaways

  • The character of the Holy Prophet ﷺ is a complete role model for students.
  • Ethics, civic sense and social responsibility are part of faith.
  • Devotion and learning go together on campus.
