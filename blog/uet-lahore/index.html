Seerat Seminar and Ilm-o-Iftar at UET Lahore

Soban Attari has addressed UET Lahore students at a Seerat and motivational seminar and at an Ilm-o-Iftar seminar in the university’s main auditorium.

Photos: Soban Attari’s official Facebook page · tap a photo to view it full size

About the Session

UET Lahore’s main auditorium hosted a large Ilm-o-Iftar seminar with Soban Attari as the speaker, attended by a large number of the university’s professors and students.

The seminar was organised to address topics that matter in every student’s life: ethics, moral values, social responsibility and civic sense.

He emphasised that the character of the Holy Prophet ﷺ is the role model for everyone and the ultimate source of guidance for every department of society, including the engineering profession.

The seminar ended with Darood-o-Salam, and a souvenir was presented to the guest speaker.

Key Takeaways

  • The Seerah offers guidance for every profession.
  • Ethics and civic sense are core parts of a student’s character.
