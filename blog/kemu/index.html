Seerat-un-Nabi Conference and Gatherings at King Edward Medical University

Soban Attari has addressed doctors, students and staff at King Edward Medical University and Mayo Hospital, including as keynote speaker at a Seerat-un-Nabi ﷺ Conference.

Photos: Soban Attari’s official Facebook page · tap a photo to view it full size

About the Session

The College of Ophthalmology and Allied Vision Sciences at KEMU organised a Seerat-un-Nabi ﷺ Conference, attended by the Principal, senior professors, doctors and students. As keynote speaker, Soban Attari spoke on the life and teachings of the Holy Prophet ﷺ and encouraged everyone to follow the Seerah in both personal and professional life.

At Mayo Hospital’s main auditorium, a large Milaad gathering was attended by the Vice Chancellor of KEMU, Professor Qazi Saeed, along with many doctors and staff.

He also spoke at a gathering for university employees in KEMU’s main auditorium, with a talk on the Sunnah, dua, and the distribution of booklets and gifts.

Key Takeaways

  • Follow the Seerah in both personal and professional life.
  • Faith and service go together in the medical profession.
